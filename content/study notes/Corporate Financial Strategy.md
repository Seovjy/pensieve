### Capital Budgeting for Efficiency
###### The Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)
- Days Payable Outstanding (DPO): From receipt of raw materials to the payment for the purchased materials.
- Days Sales of Inventory (DSI): From receipt of raw materials to delivery to customer
- Days Sales of Outstanding (DSO): From delivery to customer to collection of accounts receivable

![[CFS.png]]

$$
CCC = DSI + DSO - DPO
$$

The shorter the CCC, the better. It means your cash is tied up for a shorter time. It is a liquidity indicator of your free money for growth.

$$
DSI = \frac {\text{Inventory}}{\frac{\text{Cost of Sales}}{365}}
$$

$$
DSO = \frac {\text{Accounts Receivable}}{\frac{\text{Revenue}}{365}}
$$

$$
DPO = \frac {\text{Accounts Payable}}{\frac{\text{Total Cash Operating Expenses}}{365}}
$$
