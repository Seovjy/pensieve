Hi there! I am Jadyn. Welcome to my pensieve.

[Pensieve](https://www.harrypotter.com/writing-by-jk-rowling/pensieve) was created by combining the words: "pensive" and "sieve"—meaning a pool sorting out what is important and thoughtful. 

My motivation originates from the [digital garden](https://jzhao.xyz/posts/networked-thought) concept that I stumbled upon one day, in which I deeply resonated with. 

I am looking into my life experience in a networked way. While my life priorities may change in different phases, it doesn't mean they disappear; instead, they accumulate.

If you find yourself curious about mingling ideas, feel free to dive in!
