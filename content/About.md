### Manual
The design here follows what I consider best for reading and thinking. I keep it minimal, aiming for a clean, book-like feel while using only necessary tools to have a consistent experience across all devices.

**Toolbox and navigation** 
You can use the search feature to find specific topics or discover entries at random. You can also toggle between Light and Dark modes for your reading comfort.

**Structure and connections** 
On desktop, the Table of Contents outlines each article's sub-topics. Building on this, I connect ideas through internal links whenever related concepts appear across different posts. These tinted hyperlinks include both internal references and category tags.

These linkages help organize my digital brain while sparking new connections. If a topic catches your interest, feel free to explore the Backlinks section to dive deeper.
