When I am building my site, I have ideas about what I have accumulated and would like to share. But there are moments I wonder, why a website?

Website is a place we are able to represent ourselves, to link to people beyond boundaries. Though we can do that through other several digital platforms we have today, your website is a place you can **curate the design and build a knowledge universe of your own** without algorithmic interruptions or others' commercial purposes. 

From content to structure, I value how I weave the words into meaningful contexts and deliver them through a personalized space. Being both the author and the architect can feel overwhelming at times, but the more I work on it, the clearer my direction becomes.

I want this space to be concise, where readers don't have to guess my meaning. 

I want it to be unique and reflective, offering fresh perspectives to myself and others. 

I view it as craftsmanship. It doesn't mean every piece must be fully polished for publishing, but it has to be crafted with intention, whether finished or in progress. That care is what compounds into the very uniqueness and clarity I envision.
