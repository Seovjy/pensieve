## Liquidity Ratios
### Current ratio
measures short-term debt-paying ability
$$
\frac{\text{Current assets}}{\text{Current liabilities}}
$$

### Inventory turnover 
measures liquidity of inventory
$$
\frac{\text{Cost of goods sold}}{\text{Average Inventory}}
$$
## Profitability Ratios
### Profit margin
measures net income generated by each currency unit of sales

$$
\frac{\text{Net income}}{\text{Net sales}}
$$

### Asset turnover 
measures how efficiently assets are used to generate sales

$$
\frac{\text{Net sales}}{\text{Average total assets}}
$$

### Earnings per share (EPS)
measures net income earned on each ordinary share

$$
\frac{\text{Net income - Preference dividend}}{\text{Weighted-average 
ordinary shares outstanding}}
$$

### Price-earnings (P-E) ratio
measures the ratio of the market price per share to earnings per share

$$
\frac{\text{Market price per share}}{\text{EPS}}
$$

### Payout ratio
measures percentage of earnings distributed as cash dividends

$$
\frac{\text{Cash dividends declared on ordinary shares}}{\text{Net income}}
$$

## Solvency Ratios
### Debt to assets ratio
measures the percentage of total assets provided by creditors

$$
\frac{\text{Total liabilities}}{\text{Total assets}}
$$
